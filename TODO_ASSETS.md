# Medical case assets to source

All six current files are deliberately labelled placeholders. Before release, replace them with real medical images whose reuse terms have been verified, update each `assets.manifest.json`, and retain the visible attribution caption in the case UI.

Preferred source: Wikimedia Commons (public domain, CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA). Radiopaedia material may only be used when the specific case/image license explicitly permits this reuse.

## `case_001` — The Long Flight

- `pe_cxr_normal`: normal frontal chest radiograph. Suggested Commons search: `chest radiograph normal PA`.
- `pe_ecg_tachycardia`: 12-lead sinus tachycardia ECG; an S1Q3T3 example is optional. Suggested search: `sinus tachycardia ECG`.
- `pe_ctpa_filling_defect`: axial CTPA with a pulmonary arterial filling defect or saddle embolus. Suggested search: `CT pulmonary angiogram pulmonary embolism`.
- Optional: open-licensed leg Doppler image demonstrating non-compressible DVT.

## `case_002` — The Tearing Pain

- `dissection_cxr_wide_mediastinum`: frontal chest radiograph with a widened mediastinum. Suggested search: `widened mediastinum chest x-ray`.
- `dissection_ct_flap`: axial CT angiogram with an aortic dissection flap. Suggested search: `aortic dissection CT`.
- `dissection_ecg`: 12-lead ECG without an acute STEMI pattern, ideally sinus tachycardia/LVH.

For every replacement, record the direct source URL, author/creator, exact license and version, required attribution text, and useful alt text. Do not use an image if any of those facts cannot be verified.
